Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information ConvoGen AI collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. ConvoGen AI is operated by Akshay B K, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, and a securely hashed password managed by our authentication provider.
  • Content you provide: your chat messages, uploaded files and photos, image prompts, and hardware project requests.
  • Generated content: AI replies, generated and edited images and files, and hardware project packages created for you.
  • Usage information: plan usage counters (for example images generated today), and basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service.
  • Payment records: order and payment identifiers and payment status from Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details are handled by Razorpay directly and never reach our servers.

2. How we use it

To provide the service (including generating the replies, images, files and projects you request), to operate your plan and its limits, to send essential account emails such as verification and password resets, to provide support, and to keep the service secure.

3. Who processes it for us

We use trusted processors, each receiving only what it needs:

  • Supabase - account authentication and database.
  • Render - application hosting.
  • AI providers (such as OpenAI, Google, Groq, OpenRouter and NVIDIA) - your prompts and relevant message context are sent to generate replies.
  • Search providers (such as Tavily) - when you enable web search, your search query is sent to fetch sources.
  • Pollinations - image prompts, to generate or edit images.
  • Resend - delivery of account emails.
  • Razorpay - payment processing.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your conversations for advertising.

4. Cookies

We use only essential cookies that keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time via the Contact page; deletion is completed within 30 days, except records we must retain for legal or accounting reasons (such as payment records).

6. Security

Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider, access to data is restricted, and traffic to the service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and raise a grievance, by contacting us. We respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

8. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13. Users under 18 should use it with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version will always be on this page.
