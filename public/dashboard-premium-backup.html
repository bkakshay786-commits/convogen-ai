Hardware Studio

H-ADE turns a plain-English description into a tested ESP32 firmware project — generated, simulated and self-healed by AI, then packed as a downloadable ZIP.

Supports: ESP32 with a DHT22 sensor, relay modules, LEDs and buzzers — the AI designs the pin map and the rules from your words. Give numbers for thresholds (e.g. above 32°C): if a part isn't supported or a number is missing, H-ADE asks instead of guessing.

Takes about 30 seconds to 3 minutes — the AI tests and repairs its own firmware.
