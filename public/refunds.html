Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. What you are buying

ConvoGen AI plans (Pro at Rs. 499 and Premium at Rs. 999) are digital services. A plan is activated on your account immediately after a successful payment and stays active for 30 days. Nothing physical is shipped; see our Delivery Policy.

2. Cancellation

Plans are one-time purchases, not subscriptions, so there is no recurring charge to cancel and no auto-renewal. If you choose not to buy again, your account simply returns to the Free plan when the paid period ends. You keep full access to the paid plan until the last day of its 30-day period.

3. When you get a full refund

  • Charged but not activated: if your payment succeeded but the plan upgrade did not apply to your account because of a technical fault, we will either activate the plan or refund the full amount - your choice.
  • Duplicate payment: if you were charged twice for the same upgrade, the duplicate charge is refunded in full.
  • Failed payment: if a payment failed but money was debited, the amount is returned by your bank or Razorpay under their standard timelines; contact us if it has not arrived within 10 business days.

4. Change of mind

Because a plan activates instantly and its allowances become available immediately, change-of-mind refunds are handled case by case. Write to us within 7 days of purchase: if the upgraded allowances have not been substantially used, we will normally refund the full amount; if they have been substantially used, a refund may be reduced or declined. We reply to every refund request within 3 business days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed through Razorpay to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank account used for the payment). After we initiate a refund, banks typically take 5-7 business days to credit it.

6. How to request

Email us from your registered email address via the Contact page with your Razorpay payment ID or order ID (shown in the app and in Razorpay's payment confirmation email).
